# Ki-PIDA validation board: `kipida_test_4l`

Test structures with **hand-calculable** DC results, to validate the Ki-PIDA power-integrity simulation (and, later, thermal simulation with the heater).

> Generated by `tools/generate_kipida_test_4l.py` together with the board, the schematic and the Ki-PIDA config. Change the structures or the stackup there and re-run; do not edit this file by hand.

## Assumptions

* Copper resistivity rho = 1.68e-05 Ohm*mm (20 C), the value Ki-PIDA uses.
* Sheet resistance: outer layers (35 um) **0.480 mOhm/sq**; inner layers (15.2 um) **1.105 mOhm/sq**.
* Vias: 0.3 mm drill, 0.6 mm pad, 25 um plating -> barrel copper 0.0216 mm^2. Barrel length from copper mid-plane to mid-plane: F->B 1.5512 mm (**1.207 mOhm/via**).
* Resistance = sheet resistance x squares (length / width). Expected drops are between the two **Kelvin sense pads** (pad 2 of each terminal), not across the whole net: force pads and leads are excluded, so the formula is exact where the current is uniform.
* Temperature effects are ignored (copper +0.39 %/K). Keep real measurements short or at low current.

## How to run the validation

1. Open `pcb/kipida_test_4l/kipida_test_4l.kicad_pcb` in the KiCad PCB Editor.
2. Start Ki-PIDA: the saved config loads the rails, sources and loads above.
3. Mesh resolution: 0.1 mm is enough for every structure (all within 0.2 % of the 0.025 mm result except the vias, E1-E3: up to 3.6 %).
4. Run, then hover over each **sense pad** on the 2D map (F.Cu; In1.Cu for C1) and compare the voltage difference with **Expected drop**.

## Real-board measurements (optional)

* Drive current through the force pads (pad 1) with a bench supply or electronic load; measure voltage between the sense pads (pad 2) with a multimeter (4-wire / Kelvin).
* Expect +-10-15 % from copper-thickness tolerance and plating on the outer layers; heating raises resistance 0.39 %/K, so keep high-current measurements short.
* Parts carry an **`Imax`** field (current through the terminal at the test condition) for future Ki-PIDA load import.
